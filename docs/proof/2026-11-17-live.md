## Real-world finding: precomputed compactions

A long working session (Claude Code 2.1.326) had 8 compactions. 4 of them had `compactMetadata.precomputed: true`: PreCompact fired when the summary was built (T0), and the compaction was applied 22 minutes to 1 h 44 min later (T1), with the messages from T0 to T1 kept word for word (`preservedSegment` starts at T0).

- On hooks 1.10.0, such a compaction (applied 2026-11-17 14:11Z, 30 minutes before 1.10.1 went live) got `HANDOFF RECOVERED: the pre-compaction hook did not run`. That label was false. The content was still the session's own: `right_agent` and `delivered` passed.
- Hooks 1.10.1 (v0.8.1, spec F51) inject the T0 handoff normally, with a line that says when the compaction was prepared.
